## PokerPloker
When the game is runned (PAY ATTENTION: The game only works when it's played on full screen), a screen with a logo and a button<br> that says 'play' will show up. Click on the button that says 'play'.<br>
When clicked, another small menue appears. This is the options menu, with sadly a lot of options that do nothing, <br>but in my free time I will try to make the online and jokerpoker options work. But for now click on the button that says 'poker' under 'offline'.<br>
When clicked an option page for the game will appear. Here player names, a starting amount of money and a starting bet can be <br>filled in.<br>
Click on 'add player' to create one with the chosen name. <br>
Also, by clicking on 'bots', bots can be added. A name can be chosen from a pre-made list and added by clicking on 'add bot'.<br>
There is a maximum of 8 players/bots.<br>
When all everything is set, click on 'Start' to start the game.<br>
A new screen will be shown. This is the begin setup of the game.<br>
There are three buttons on the bottom left: 'Fold', 'Check' and 'Raise'. These buttons can be clicked during the game to indicate <br>the prefered actions.<br>
At the bottom right there will be symbols that look like this: [?][?]. These are the players cards. By default they are hidden<br>
When the player wishes to look at their cards, the player can click the button under these symbols that says 'Show cards'.<br>
When this button is clicked, each '?' will change into one of the following four letters: S (Spades), H (Hearts), D (Diamonds) or <br>C (Clubs), followed by a number from 2 to 14 (with 11 being the Jack, 12 the Queen, 13 the King and 14 the Ace).<br>
For example: [?][?] 'Show cards' is clicked: [S3][H12]. (the cards are 3 of Spades and Queen of Hearts).<br>
On the left side, there is a small player overview with their name, amount of money/chips and their bets.<br>
In the middle of the screen 5 more symbols are shown: [?][?][?][?][?]. These are the 5 table cards that will be shown throuhgout <br>the game.<br>
In the top right there is a button that says 'Start new round'.<br>
Click this button to start a new game round.<br>
In the middle the first three table cards will be shown (in the same way as how they player cards are shown).<br>
For example: [D6][H2][C9][?][?].<br>
A little under the table cards, the computer tells the players which one's turn it is. ("It's Player1 turn").<br>
This player sits behind the computer and looks at the visible cards. (The once on the table and in their hand).<br>
Based on these cards, the player makes the decision if they want to fold, check or raise and clicks on the button that states <br>their actions.<br>
If the player choses to raise, the player can type in the wished amount with what they want to raise in the small type box above<br> the raise button. <br>
This amount will be added up with their starting bet and the new amount, is shown in the player overview.<br>
Then, the computer will say which player is next and these steps repeat itself.<br>
After all players have made their decision, the next round begins.<br>
Which means that the fourth tablecard is shown and the players can make their decisions again in the same way as before.<br>
After that, the fifth and last tablecard is shown and the players can make one final choice.<br>
After all rounds are finished, the players will check who has won by the standard Texas hold'm rules and select a winner.<br>
They can insert who won, by clicking the button 'select winner' in the top right corner and select the playername of the winner.<br>
The round can end earlier if all but one player folds, that player is the winner of the round.<br>
After the winner is selected the winner receives the amount of money they won.<br>
The players can chose to play another round by clicking the button 'Start new round'.<br>
If they do so, they will continue with the data of the previous round.<br>
Meaning that the winner will also have the money they won in the previous round.<br>
This next round and all that will come after, are played in the same way as explained above.<br>
