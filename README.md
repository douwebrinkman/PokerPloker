## PokerPloker
When the game is runned (PAY ATTENTION: The game only works when it's played on full screen), a screen with a logo and a button that says 'play' will show up. Click on the button that says 'play'. 
When clicked, another small menue appears. Click on the button that says 'poker' under 'offline'. 
When clicked an option page for the game will appear. Here player names, a starting amount of money and a starting bet can be filled in.
Click on 'add player' to create one with the chosen name. 
Also, by clicking on 'bots', bots can be added. A name can be chosen from a pre-made list and added by clicking on 'add bot'.
There is a maximum of 8 players/bots.
When all everything is set, click on 'Start' to start the game.
A new screen will be shown. This is the begin setup of the game.
There are three buttons on the bottom left: 'Fold', 'Check' and 'Raise'. These buttons can be clicked during the game to indicate the prefered actions.
At the bottom right there will be symbols that look like this: [?][?]. These are the players cards. Right now they are hidden.
When the player wishes to look at their cards, the player can click the button under these symbols that says 'Show cards'.
When this button is clicked, each '?' will change into one of the following four letters: S (Spades), H (Hearts), D (Diamonds) or C (Clubs), followed by a number from 2 to 14 (with 11 being the Jack, 12 the Queen, 13 the King and 14 the Ace).
For example: [?][?] 'Show cards' is clicked: [S3][H12]. (Cards are 3 of Spades and Queen of Hearts).
On the left side, there is a small player overview with their name, amount of money/chips and their bets.
In the middle of the screen 5 more symbols are shown: [?][?][?][?][?]. These are the 5 table cards that will be shown throuhgout the game.
In the top right there is a button that says 'Start new round'.
Click this button to start a new game round.
In the middle the first three table cards will be shown (in the same way as how they player cards are shown).
For example: [D6][H2][C9][?][?].
A little under the table cards, the computer tells the players which one's turn it is. ("It's Player1 turn").
This player sits behind the computer and looks at the visible cards. (The once on the table and in their hand).
Based on these cards, the player makes the decision if they want to fold, check or raise and clicks on the button that states their actions.
If the player choses to raise, the player can type in the wished amount with what they want to raise in the small type box that shows up when the button 'raise' is clicked.
This amount will be added up with their starting bet and the new amount, is shown in the player overview.
Then, the computer will say which player is next and these steps repeat itself.
After all players have made their decision, the next round begins.
Which means that the fourth tablecard is shown and the players can make their decisions again in the same way as before.
After that, the fifth and last tablecard is shown and the players can make one final choice.

After all rounds are finished, the players will check who has won by the standard Texas hold'm rules and select a winner.
They can insert who won, by clicking the button 'select winner' in the top right corner and select the playername of the winner.
After the winner is selected the winner receives the amount of money they won.
The players can chose to play another round by clicking the button 'Start new round'.
If they do so, they will continue with the data of the previous round.
Meaning that the winner will also have the money they won in the previous round.
This next round and all that will come after, are played in the same way as explained above.
